    Frequently Asked Questions

    Find clear answers to common questions about our installations, equipment packages, and ongoing support.

    Do you sell the hardware/packages separately, or does it include installation?

    We offer both! You can purchase standalone tech packages and equipment directly from us, or opt for our end-to-end service which includes professional delivery, expert installation, configuration, and testing.

    : What locations do you service for installations?

    We are based in Ghana and provide professional on-site installation, setup, and maintenance services nationwide for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

    How do I request a site visit or a custom quote?

    You can easily reach out to us via our Contact Page, phone call, or WhatsApp. For complex properties or large commercial spaces, we schedule a quick site assessment to provide an accurate, tailored quotation.

    Do I need an internet connection for my CCTV system to work?

    No. Your security cameras will still record and store footage locally onto your DVR or NVR storage hard drive without the internet. However, an active internet connection is required if you want to view live feeds or receive motion alert notifications remotely on your smartphone.

    Can I answer my gate intercom and unlock my door using my smartphone when I'm not at home?

    Yes, with our smart access control solutions, you can answer your gate intercom and unlock your door remotely using your smartphone. This feature is particularly useful for managing access for guests or service providers when you're away from home.
